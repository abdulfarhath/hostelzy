Delete your Hostelzy account

You can delete your account and your data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Hostelzy.
  2. Tap Me (or the menu), then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and follow the steps.

If you have an open hold or an unpaid owner plan, the app tells you what to settle first.

If you signed in with Google, the app asks you to confirm with Google once more, then deletes your account on our servers and your Google sign-in with Hostelzy. If you used Hostelzy on this phone only, it removes everything from the phone.

Without the app

Send us a WhatsApp message from the phone number on your account, saying "Delete my Hostelzy account". We reply to confirm and delete it within 30 days.

WhatsApp +91 90597 90014

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone and role.
  • Your enquiries, holds, reviews, complaints and notification tokens.
  • Your Google sign-in link with Hostelzy.

Payment records an owner needs for their accounts are kept as long as Indian law requires, without your profile.
