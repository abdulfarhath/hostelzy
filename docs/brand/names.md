# Name ideas (round 1, 2026-10-02)

Brand chat. **Ideas only. Nothing is picked.** The founder decides.
Nothing in the app is renamed until the founder says so.

## What a good name must do
- Say "find and hold a bed in a PG / hostel" and "you can trust it".
- Easy to say for Telugu, Hindi and English speakers.
- Easy to spell after hearing it once.

## How we checked (be honest)
| Check | Done? |

| .in domains | **No** (lookup blocked here) |

| Play Store / App Store | Partly: web search of store pages only. Direct store search is blocked here. |
| IP India trademark search | **No** (blocked here) |

(classes 9, 42, 43, 36).

## Top 3 (Brand chat's suggestion)

| Rank | Name | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cotkey** | Short, 2 sounds, same in every language. "Key" = trust + getting in. Works well with a block logo (a key-shaped or keyhole bed block). |
| 2 | **Bedpakka** | Says the exact promise: your bed is **pakka** (confirmed). Feels local to Hyderabad but English speakers can still say it. |
| 3 | **Bedmila** | The happiest moment for a tenant: "Bed mila!" Great in reels and ads. Weaker for Telugu-only and English speakers. |

Runner-up: **Cotwise** (easy, but says "choose", not "hold"; and "Wise" is a big brand).

Bedpakka and Bedmila are the same style (a Hindi phrase). If the founder likes that style,
keep one of them, not both.

# Round 2: names like "Hostelzy" (2026-10-02)

Founder: "Hostelzy sounded good. Can there be something like this? It's not all about bed."
So round 2 = whole-stay names, friendly ending, like Hostelzy.

## First: why "Hostelzy" itself is risky
- **Hostelz.com** is a hostel search site (since 2002, 35,000+ listings, has an app).
- "Hostelzy" = "Hostelz" + "y". Same business. One letter apart.
- hostelzy.com and .app are free, but that does not make the name safe.
- A lawyer may say it is too close. **Don't build the brand on it until a lawyer checks.**
- Also: "hostel" alone is a common word. Only the ending (zy, ji…) can be protected.

## Brand chat's picks (round 2)
1. **Hostelji**: closest feel to Hostelzy, warm, easy for everyone, .com free.
2. **Staypakka**: says the promise (trust), about the whole stay, .com free.
3. **Cotkey** (from round 1): still works, "key" = your way into a stay, not only a bed.
