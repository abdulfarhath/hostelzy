1 · Logo

The room, from above

{{MARK_FULL}}

A real PG room seen from the ceiling, drawn the way the app’s Room tab draws it. The story: see the real room, pick your bed. It is logo B3-a2, picked by the founder on 2 Oct 2026.

  • Walls, window, door gap, AC with air, fan: ink.
  • Other beds (a bunk and one bed): grey.
  • Your bed: red. The only red in the logo.
  • Floor: always see-through. The room sits on whatever ground is behind it.

Wordmark: hostelzy, lowercase, Archivo ExtraBold, −0.02em, as in the app header. In the lockup the room is 1.18× the height of the “h”, with a gap of 0.3×.

App and Play icon

Two levels of detail

At 64 px and smaller, use the simple room: the AC becomes a bar, the fan a dot, no pillows or air dots. The status bar uses its own one-colour drawing: beds as outlines, your bed filled.

Clear space: keep empty space of at least one wall-to-bed gap (about 12% of the room’s width) on every side. Smallest sizes: room 16 px, lockup 24 px tall.

4 · Tone of voice

Like a friend who has stayed in PGs

  • Plain words. Short sentences. Words people in Ameerpet use: PG, sharing, advance, bed, owner.
  • Honest. Say only what really happened. “Paid”, “Sent” or “Verified” only when it is true.
  • Say why. When we ask for something, give the reason in the same line.
  • Warm, not cute. No slang, no emoji in the app, no exclamation marks except “Bed mila!”.

5 · Do and don’t

Keep the room as it is

  • Do use the files from docs/brand/assets/ as they are. Square corners, 2px rules, flat colour.
  • Do put the room on the app ground (light or dark) or white, with clear space around it.
  • Do keep the wordmark as live text in the app; use the outlined lockup elsewhere.
{{DONTS}}
  • Don’t stretch, outline, add a gradient, put text inside the room or draw a new room.
  • Don’t write the name as “HostelZy”, “Hostel-zy” or in capitals in running text. In sentences: Hostelzy. In the logo: hostelzy.
