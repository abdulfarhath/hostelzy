Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026. Hostelzy is run by its founder in Hyderabad, India. Questions: WhatsApp +91 90597 90014.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email, when you sign in with Google.
  • Your phone number, which you type in. We don’t check it by SMS yet, so the app shows it as “not verified”.
  • Your role: tenant, resident or hostel owner.
  • What you do in Hostelzy: enquiries, bed holds, bookings, payment references (UTR numbers you type; we never see your bank or UPI PIN), complaints, reviews, Fair Play reports, and, for owners, hostel details, rooms, beds, rents and residents.
  • Approximate location, only when you tap “Use my location” on the map. It is used on your phone to show hostels near you. It is not sent to Hostelzy or stored on our servers.
  • A notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications, so we can send you alerts (for example “your hold was confirmed”).
  • Crash reports (device model, Android version, and what the app was doing when it crashed), through Firebase Crashlytics, so we can fix bugs.

Where it is stored

  • Supabase (database, Mumbai region, India): your profile and what you do in Hostelzy.
  • Google Firebase: sign-in, notification tokens and crash reports.
  • On your phone: your login and settings, so you stay signed in.

Who sees it

  • When you enquire about, hold or book a bed, that hostel’s owner and managers see your name, phone number and HZ code.
  • If you live in a hostel, its owner sees your rent payments and complaints.
  • The Hostelzy team sees what it needs to run the service and to check Fair Play cases.
  • We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account we delete your data within 30 days. We keep only what Indian law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

Your choices

  • Turn off location or notifications any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Change your name and phone in the app.
