What Hostelzy is

Hostelzy helps you find PG and hostel beds in Hyderabad, hold a bed before you visit, and, once you live there, pay rent and raise complaints. Owners use it to run their hostel. Hostelzy is not the owner of any hostel; your stay is an agreement between you and the hostel.

Holds and payments

  • A free hold keeps a bed for a short time (shown in the app) and then ends on its own.
  • You pay the hostel owner directly by UPI. Hostelzy never takes your money for rent or advance. A payment is “Paid” only after the owner confirms it.
  • Owners pay Hostelzy a flat monthly plan by bed count. There is no commission.

Be fair

  • Give your real name and phone number.
  • Owners must list real beds, real prices and real photos, and add residents who came through Hostelzy within 3 days of moving in.
  • Hostelzy’s Fair Play team looks into reports and can warn, pause or remove a hostel or an account.

Test builds

The app is in testing. Things can change or break. Don’t rely on it as your only record of payments.
