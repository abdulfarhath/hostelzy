# Owner pitch numbers

Owned by Finance. **Only the maths.** The words, scripts and posters are the Marketing chat's
(`docs/marketing/`). Prices are from DECISIONS (2026-10-02). Updated 2026-10-02.

**Honesty rule:** these show what the plan **costs**. We never promise tenants or a number of
filled beds. Say "if", never "you will".

## 1. What the plan costs per day

| Hostel size | Plan | Per day | Per bed per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 30 beds | ₹499/mo | **₹17 a day** | ₹17 (30 beds) |
| 31–80 beds | ₹999/mo | **₹33 a day** | ₹12.50 (80 beds) |
| 80+ beds | ₹1,499/mo + featured spot | **₹50 a day** | ₹12.50 (120 beds) |

First **30 days free**. No card. No fee per tenant. No cut of the rent. The advance goes straight to the owner.

## 2. "How soon does it pay for itself?"

Days one empty bed has to be filled to cover a month of the plan
(= plan ÷ (bed rent ÷ 30)):

| Bed rent / month | ₹499 plan | ₹999 plan | ₹1,499 plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹5,000 | 3 days | 6 days | 9 days |
| ₹7,000 | **2 days** | 4.3 days | 6.4 days |
| ₹9,000 | 1.7 days | 3.3 days | 5 days |

**Line to say:** "If Hostelzy fills just one bed two days sooner, the month is paid for."

## 3. What one empty bed costs the owner

| Bed rent | One bed empty for 1 month | = months of the ₹499 plan |
|---|---|---|
| ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 lost | 10 months |
| ₹7,000 | ₹7,000 lost | 14 months |
| ₹9,000 | ₹9,000 lost | 18 months |

## 4. Ask the owner, don't claim

- "How many beds were empty last month?" → multiply by their rent. That is their real loss.
- "What do you pay a broker or a listing site today?" → compare with section 1. Don't quote
  competitor prices we haven't checked.

- Extras that cost no cash: **Founding hostel** badge, featured spot for 3 months.
