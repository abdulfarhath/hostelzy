# Marketing

Owned by the **Hostelzy · Marketing** chat. The founder talks marketing there; plans, scripts and
posters are written here.

## Ground rules (founder + Ideas chat, 2026-10-02)
- **Honest only.** No fake or dummy hostels, no made-up numbers ("X tenants got a bed through us")
  and no listing a hostel without its owner's OK. Hostelzy's whole brand is trust; one fake listing
  found by an owner or tenant can end it in an area. Misleading ads can also break Indian
  consumer-protection law.
- **Proof we can show truthfully:** the tenant waitlist count, founding hostels (with permission),
  real enquiries, real owner quotes.
- **Posters** only where we have permission: gates of hostels that joined, tea stalls, mess,
  laundries, Xerox shops, coaching-centre notice boards. Not on other people's walls (GHMC fines).
- Writing style: very simple English, short lines, tables, examples. The founder reads on a phone.

## Starting plan (from the Ideas chat)
1. **Founding 20:** friends introduce their hostel owners. Offer: free 3 months, then ₹299/mo
   locked for life, featured spot. "You'll be one of the first 20 hostels in Ameerpet."
2. **Tenant waitlist first:** Instagram page + Google Form. "312 students are looking for PGs in
   your area" is the number that sells to owners.
3. **Demo honestly:** the app's sample hostels are shown as "this is how your hostel will look".
4. **Guarantee:** "No enquiry in 30 days → you never pay."
5. **Reels:** honest PG tours, advance horror stories, AC vs non-AC price reveals, "5 questions
   before you pay the advance", founder story. 4–5 a week, every reel ends "Join the waitlist".
6. **Cheap channels:** coaching / college / IT new-joiner WhatsApp and Telegram groups, r/hyderabad,
   campus ambassadors (₹100 per owner), ₹100 referral (F09), tiffin/laundry/coaching partners,
   Google Maps business profile.
7. **4 weeks:** W1 Instagram + waitlist + 5 reels + friends' owners · W2 5 founding hostels +
   stickers · W3 10 more using waitlist + references · W4 20 hostels, launch to tenants.
