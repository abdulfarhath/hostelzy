# 30-day launch plan: first 20 PGs + first tenants

Owned by Marketing. Written 2026-10-03. Reads with `docs/finance/plan.md` (budget, Month 1 targets),
`owner-pitch.md` (what to say), `tenant-growth.md` (tenant channels), `reels.md` (content).

**Goal by Day 30:** 20 PGs live on Hostelzy (on the 30-day free trial), 300 people on the tenant
waitlist, first 20 bed holds. These are **targets, not promises** (Finance §2).

Why this order: DECISIONS (2026-10-02) puts the first clusters at **Ameerpet / SR Nagar** and
**Madhapur / Hitec City / Kondapur**, ~10 hostels each. The app already groups **KPHB with Ameerpet**
and **Gachibowli with Madhapur**, so they are the edges of the same two clusters. Dense first: 10 PGs
in one area beat 20 spread thin, because tenants search one area at a time.

**Who** (Month 1): **F** = founder. **H** = a helper (friend, or a sales partner once one signs the
agreement in `docs/finance/hiring-post.md`; partners are paid only when an owner pays, so ₹0 in Month 1).

## What "signed" means

A PG counts when **all** of these are true (the app's own go-live checklist, F14/F24):

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Owner said yes and accepted the Fair Play rules in the app | DECISIONS: rules shown and accepted when the owner joins |
| Rooms, sharing × AC/non-AC rates, food, house rules, photos and map pin are in | A listing goes live only when complete |
| Room layouts drawn (we draw them on the visit, free) | Tenants pick a bed from the layout |
| Owner account linked and the owner can open the app | The owner gets holds and enquiries |
| Current residents added (name + phone) | Needed to tell "Via Hostelzy" from "Direct" |
| QR poster up (owner's OK) | Best tenant source (Finance §3) |

Go-live starts the **30-day free trial** and shows "Visited by Hostelzy".
