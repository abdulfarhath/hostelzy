# Owner pitch: visit script, objections, leave-behind

Owned by Marketing. Written 2026-10-03. Prices are **Plan A from DECISIONS (2026-10-02)**. Maths is
Finance's (`docs/finance/owner-pitch-numbers.md`). Features are only ones the app has today
(`docs/BOARD.md`, F13 server notes).

## 1. Rules for every visit

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Say "if", "can", "tenants can" | Say "you will get tenants" or "we fill your beds" |
| Show the app on your phone | Show a sample hostel as if it were a real one. Say "this is a sample: your PG will look like this" |
| Ask how many beds were empty last month | Quote competitor prices we haven't checked |
| Use only the prices below | Offer "₹299 for life", "never pay if no enquiry", a Founding badge or a 3-month featured spot (**pending founder**) |
| Say phone numbers are typed in, not checked by OTP yet | Say "verified by OTP" or "verified tenants" |
| Ask before putting up a poster | List a PG without the owner's OK |
| Speak Telugu / Hindi / English, whatever the owner likes. The app has Telugu too | Push. A "not now" is fine: come back once |

## 2. The visit script (10 minutes)

| Step | Time | Say (plain words) | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hello | 30 s | "Namaste sir/madam. I'm [name] from Hostelzy, a new Hyderabad app for finding PGs. [Friend] told me about your PG. Do you have 10 minutes?" | — |
| 2. Ask | 2 min | "How many beds do you have? How many were empty last month? How do tenants find you now: walk-ins, brokers, a website? What is the hardest part: empty beds, advance fights, rent follow-ups?" | Write their answers |
| 3. Their loss | 1 min | "So [N] empty beds × ₹[rent] = ₹[loss] last month." | Their own numbers |
| 4. What tenants see | 2 min | "Tenants see your rooms by sharing, AC and non-AC, the rent, food menu, house rules and the room layout: they can pick a bed. They can hold a bed free for 1 hour. You get a notification and you confirm or decline." | Explore → a hostel page → Room tab (sample, say so) |
| 5. What you get | 2 min | "Enquiries come on your WhatsApp with an HZ code, so you know they came from Hostelzy. The advance goes **straight to you** by UPI; Hostelzy never touches it. Your residents list, rent status, complaints and reviews from your own residents are in one app." | Owner side: Today, Enquiries, Residents |
| 6. Price | 1 min | "First 30 days free. No card. Then [₹499/₹999/₹1,499] a month for your size. That's about ₹[17/33/50] a day. No fee per tenant. No cut of your rent. If Hostelzy fills just one bed two days sooner, the month is paid for." | `owner-pitch-numbers.md` table |
| 7. The rules | 1 min | "One rule matters: add every resident in the app. If a tenant finds you on Hostelzy and joins, please don't take them off the app. That's how tenants trust Hostelzy, and it keeps the deals fair for you too." | Fair Play rules screen |
| 8. Ask | 30 s | "Shall we put your PG on now? It takes about 30–40 minutes: rooms, rates, photos. I draw your room layouts free." | Add hostel tool |
| 9. Close | 30 s | Yes → onboard now. Not now → "Can I come back on [day]?" + give the leave-behind. Always: "Do you know another owner I should meet?" | Leave-behind |

## 3. Objections and answers

| Owner says | Answer (honest) |
|---|---|
| "I'm already full." | "Great. Do you get empty beds when people leave in March–June or after exams? Put it on free for 30 days. When a bed frees up, tenants can already see you." |
| "I get tenants from walk-ins / board outside." | "Keep that. Hostelzy is one more door. Walk-ins see one PG; Hostelzy tenants compare rooms, so a clean, honest PG stands out." |
| "Brokers bring me tenants." | "What do you pay them per tenant? Hostelzy is a flat ₹[price] a month, no fee per tenant, no cut of rent." (Don't guess their broker fee; let them say it.) |
| "Apps take a cut / hold my money." | "Hostelzy never holds money. The advance goes from the tenant straight to your UPI. You confirm you got it in the app." |
| "₹499 is too much." | "That's ₹17 a day. One empty bed for a month costs you ₹[rent]. And the first 30 days are free: decide after you've seen it." |
| "What if nobody enquires?" | "Then don't pay after the trial. There's no contract and no card; you just stop." (Don't offer "never pay" or a free extra month: pending founder.) |
| "Why must I add every resident?" | "So Hostelzy can tell who came through the app and who came directly. Direct residents cost you nothing extra. It also lets your residents leave reviews; only people you added can review you." |
| "Tenants will contact me directly and skip the app." | "Before a hold, tenants only see 'Enquire on WhatsApp' with an HZ code. Your number shows after they hold a bed. If someone found you on Hostelzy and joins, please mark them; it's in the rules you accept." |
| "Bad reviews will hurt me." | "Only residents you added can review, after a confirmed stay. You can reply to every review. The ranking also counts reply speed and keeping beds up to date, which you control." |
| "I don't have time for an app." | "I'll set it up now with you. After that: confirm holds, tap 'still N free beds?' every few days, add new residents. Your manager can do it too." |
| "I don't know how to use it." / "English is hard." | "The app is in Telugu too. I'll show you once, and you can WhatsApp us on 90597 90014 anytime." |
| "Is my residents' data safe?" | "Your residents' names are never shown to tenants. Women's PGs: room layouts only for signed-in users, whole-floor plans only after a hold. We never show gates, CCTV or exits." |
| "Who are you? Is this a real company?" | "It's a new Hyderabad startup; I'm the founder [or: I work with the founder]. You'd be one of the first PGs in [area]. You can call me any time." (No company claims beyond that.) |
| "Will you guarantee tenants?" | "No. Nobody honestly can. I can promise your PG shows clearly, the advance stays with you, and you pay nothing for 30 days." |
| "Can I get a discount?" | "Price is the same for everyone right now. The trial is 30 days free." (Founding perks: pending founder; don't offer.) |
| "Put my PG on, but not the layout / photos." | "A PG goes live only when it's complete, so tenants trust it. I'll take the photos now: 10 minutes." |

New objection heard on a visit? Add a row here with the date.
