# Tenant growth: cheap and free channels

Owned by Marketing. Written 2026-10-03. Money: `docs/finance/plan.md` (Instagram boost ₹2,000/month
max, print ₹4,000, rewards cap ₹5,000 **PROPOSED**). Tenants pay Hostelzy nothing (DECISIONS).

## What we can honestly tell tenants

| Say | Because the app really does it |
|---|---|
| "See rooms by sharing, AC / non-AC, with the real rent" | Rate card per sharing × AC (F16) |
| "Pick your bed on the room layout" | Room tab (F12) |
| "Hold a bed free for 1 hour" | Free hold, 2 hours for Members (F04, F09) |
| "Pay the advance straight to the owner" | Hostelzy never holds money; UPI → UTR → owner confirms |
| "Know the exit rules before you pay" | ₹3,000 advance, 30 days notice, ₹1,000–1,500 maintenance shown up front |
| "Reviews only from people who stayed" | Reviews only from residents the owner added |
| "Hostelzy deals: save on the advance or rent" | Owners pick deals; only Hostelzy tenants get them (F03) |
| "₹100 off your next PG, ₹100 for each friend" | Stay Rewards (F09) |
| "Food menu, rent and water reminders" | Food menu (F24), Reminders (F20) |

**Never say:** "verified by OTP", "verified owners/tenants", "guaranteed bed", "X people found a PG
through us" (until it's a real count), "best PGs in Hyderabad". Show only PGs whose owners joined.
