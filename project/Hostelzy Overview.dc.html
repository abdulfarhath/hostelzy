Hostelzy · mobile app v1 · all screens
One app. Three roles. Every bed visible.
Open the clickable prototype →
System: Modernist. Archivo, one red, no rounded corners, 2px rules. The grid does the organising.
Roles: picked once after OTP: tenant, resident, owner. Each gets four tabs and a red action in the middle: Search, Pay rent, Add booking.
Beds: the same five states everywhere. Free (outline), free soon (dashed), on hold (hatched), taken (grey), selected (red).
Holds: free for 1 hour (owner confirms), ₹299 for 48 hours, or a ₹2,000 token to book outright. Owner contact always goes through WhatsApp.
